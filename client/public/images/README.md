## Tips

- **Landscape / wide** works best for hero and banner.
- **Portrait (4:5)** works well for city cards and gallery tall cell.
- **Square** works for Instagram strip and activity cards.
- After you drop files in, tell me in chat, e.g. *"use my `cities/marrakech.jpg` for the hero"* — or say *"wire all images from README"* and I will replace every placeholder automatically.
